# Welcome to your second brain

An assistant that remembers you. You talk, it writes down what matters, and next time it knows where you stopped.
Your notes stay on this computer.

## Start

1. In VS Code, open Claude: the Claude icon on the left side bar (or the spark icon at the top right of an open file).
2. Sign in with your Claude account (Pro or higher).
3. Say or type **"hi"**. It will explain the rest in your language.

## Talking with your voice

- **Wispr Flow** — the most accurate. Hold the shortcut it shows you, speak, release. Free: 2,000 words a week;
  after that it stops until the weekly reset (or $15 a month for unlimited).
- **Built-in dictation** — no limit, a bit less accurate. Keep it as your backup:
  - Windows: click in the chat box, press **Win + H**, speak.
  - Mac: turn it on once in System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation. Then click in the chat box and press the
    Microphone key on the top row of the keyboard (or Edit → Start Dictation). No length limit; it stops after
    30 seconds of silence.

## The voice that answers

The assistant reads its answers aloud. Say **"voice off"** to stop it, **"voice on"** to bring it back.
Any new message stops the voice that is speaking.

## How it gets better

When it gets something wrong, tell it. It writes the rule into [[My rules]] and follows it from then on.
That is the whole trick: the more you correct it, the more it becomes yours.

## What it costs and where your words go

- Claude subscription — your own account (Pro or higher). The assistant uses its limits.
- Wispr Flow — free 2,000 words a week, or paid.
- The voice — free.
- What you say goes to Anthropic (Claude) to answer; dictation goes to Wispr Flow or Microsoft/Apple; the text of
  answers goes to Microsoft to be turned into speech. Your notes stay on this computer. Nothing is sent to the person
  who set this up for you.

## Don't

- Don't paste passwords, card numbers or ID numbers into the chat.

## Where things are

This app (Obsidian) shows your notes nicely — open the graph view to see how they connect.
You don't need to manage anything: the assistant files everything itself.
